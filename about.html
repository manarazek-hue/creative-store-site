ABOUT

I work where creativity and technology meet.

Music, software, artificial intelligence, automation and digital products are different parts of the same work: turning ideas into systems that actually do something.

HOW I WORK

I don't build isolated AI experiments. I build systems.

My work moves between creative production and technology.

On one side, I develop music, visual concepts, video workflows and digital content.

On the other, I build software, automation, AI systems and tools designed around real workflows and decisions.

The overlap is where things become interesting: using technology to improve creative work, and using creative projects as real environments for testing technology.
