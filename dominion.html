CONCEPT / R&D · ORIGINAL IP

DOMINION: WORLD//WAR

Every Territory Has a Price. Every Alliance Has an End.

CONCEPT RECORD

Date of creation: 19 September 2026

This page documents the original concept, name, premise and core mechanics of DOMINION: WORLD//WAR by Mana, first published on the date above. All rights reserved.

Status: Concept / R&D. Development stage: pre-production.

Current objective: define the core identity, gameplay loop, economy and technical architecture before implementation.

Blockchain: experimental / undecided. Token: not yet defined. Final combat mathematics are intentionally not locked.

Design principle: build the game first, the economy second, and add blockchain only where it genuinely improves ownership, competition or player interaction.

GENRE

Persistent grand strategy

Online persistent grand strategy, territory conquest and multiplayer geopolitical strategy.

DOMINION: WORLD//WAR is a persistent online strategy game where players compete for control of a living global map. Players establish territories, build military strength, form alliances, negotiate treaties, attack neighbours, defend borders and compete for dominance.

The world does not reset after every match. The world remembers. Your victories affect borders. Your betrayals affect relationships. Your alliances create political blocs. Your expansion makes you a target.

The objective is not simply to own the most land. It is to become a power that other players cannot ignore.

THE WORLD

A fictionalised Earth

Hundreds or thousands of strategically important territories, each with its own population, resources, strategic value, terrain, economic potential, defensive advantages and political importance.

The world evolves with player activity. A quiet territory today could become the centre of tomorrow's war.

Characters: there is no traditional protagonist. The players are the characters, each the leader of an emerging power. Later additions may include AI-controlled nations, faction leaders, generals, diplomats, mercenaries, traders, intelligence agents and political factions.

CORE MECHANICS & UNIQUE HOOKS

Concept-stage design

Persistent World

One continuously living global map. Players take part in an evolving geopolitical world, not isolated matches.

Territory Warfare

Players attack neighbouring territories to expand influence. Every conquest has consequences: more territory, more income, more borders, more vulnerabilities.

Strategy Over Wallet

More resources must not automatically mean winning. A smaller power can beat a larger one through positioning, timing, alliances, diplomacy, deception and resource management.

Alliances & Diplomacy

Alliances, non-aggression pacts, trade agreements, military pacts and betrayal. Player-generated political history.

Information Warfare

Scouting, espionage, misinformation, hidden troop movements, intelligence reports and fog of war. You know something is moving near your border, not how many units.

Territory Economy

Territories produce Energy, Metals, Food, Technology, Capital and Influence.

Capitals & Strategic Territories

Capitals, ports, energy hubs, industrial centres, trade routes, military strongholds and rare-resource regions. One key territory can matter more than ten ordinary ones.

Formal Wars

Declared conflicts with objectives such as capturing a capital, holding territories, destroying military infrastructure or holding ground for a set time. Wars get a beginning, escalation and conclusion.

Seasons

The world runs in seasons, for example Season 01 - The First World, then Season 02 - The Fracture, each with new conditions, map configuration and resources, so the oldest players never permanently dominate.

Optional Blockchain Layer

A layer, not the foundation. The game must stay interesting without real money. Possible uses: player-owned assets, tournament rewards, marketplace, collectibles, governance, tokenised rewards.

AI Factions

AI-controlled powers with their own objectives: economic dominance, expansion, peace, manipulation, secretly backing weaker factions. Their behaviour evolves with player actions.

Reputation

Every player builds a history: territories conquered, wars won and lost, alliances, treaties broken, trade agreements, betrayals. Not a good/bad score. The world remembers what you did.
